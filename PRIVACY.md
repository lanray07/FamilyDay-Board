# FamilyDay Board privacy policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026. This policy describes FamilyDay Board development edition 0.1.0, published by Lanray. Contact: banksmi@mail.com.

## Local household planning

The app stores household names, member nicknames and colours, calendar events, imported calendar information, notes, shopping lists, chores, completion records and preferences in its private storage on your device. Adults choose the information entered and which entries children can see. Children do not need email addresses. Core local features do not require an online account or cloud registration.

The app has no advertising, analytics or publisher-operated tracking service. This edition has no purchases or subscriptions. Lanray does not operate a hosted FamilyDay sync service in this edition and does not receive your household content through normal local use.

## Optional connected household

An adult may configure a trusted HTTPS server and device token or join using an invitation. Connected mode sends the household snapshot, including member names, plans, imported events, notes, lists, chores and completion information, to that chosen server and retrieves updates. It also uses device credentials, invitation records and sync-operation metadata to authenticate devices and coordinate changes. The parent PIN is not sent to the server.

Every authorised adult device can read and administer the connected household. The server operator controls its infrastructure, access logs, retention and backups; ask that operator about its practices before connecting. HTTPS protects network transport, but this is not end-to-end encryption against the server operator. You may continue using local features without enabling sync.

## Optional device services

Calendar import requires your permission to read device calendars. Imported information is saved as household content and is included in sync if you enable it. Local reminders use device notifications, subject to your permission and device settings.

Voice input invokes the device's optional speech-recognition service only when you request it. That service may send audio to its provider for online processing under its own privacy policy. Recognised text is shown for review before saving. The app does not maintain audio recordings. If recognition is unavailable, you can type instead. Optional text-to-speech passes selected text to the installed speech engine, whose processing depends on the chosen provider and settings.

## Backups and sharing

Adults can explicitly export household backups and share lists through the device's file and sharing controls. Backup files contain household information and should be kept private. The destinations you select have their own privacy practices. Exported files are outside the app's private storage and are not removed when you erase the app's local household.

## Children and adult controls

FamilyDay Board is a household planning tool with adult-managed child spaces. Adults create child nicknames and select the plans, notes and chores visible to them. Child spaces do not provide public profiles, public posting or a public social feed. Adults should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information and supervise optional third-party services. The parent PIN limits access within the app; it does not replace device security or protect exported files.

## Retention, access and deletion

Local household content remains until you edit or delete it, erase the household, or remove the app's local storage. Adult controls let you edit and delete individual entries. To remove the local household and parent PIN, authenticate as an adult and use Settings → Private by design → Delete household from this tablet. Verify the confirmation carefully because this action is permanent. App data backup through the operating system is disabled; explicit backups remain your responsibility.

If connected mode is enabled, disconnecting stops further sync but does not delete the server copy. Before erasing local data, use Settings → Connected household → Delete household from server to delete the active remote snapshot and revoke all connected device tokens and invitations. Other tablets' existing local copies and operator-managed server backups are not erased by that action. Delete local copies on each tablet and contact the server operator about its backups. Revoking a device blocks future server requests but cannot erase that device's offline copy.

Lanray cannot access or delete data held solely on your device, your exported files or an independently operated server. For help with access or deletion controls, contact banksmi@mail.com. Do not email household backups, parent PINs or device tokens.

## Support and changes

If you contact support, Lanray receives the email address and information you choose to include and uses them to answer your request. Email is handled by the relevant mail providers. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information; you can ask support to delete its correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Amazon separately handles Appstore distribution, account and download information under its own policies. GitHub hosts this public policy page under its own policies. Neither service's separate processing is controlled by the app.

Future versions may change these practices. Material changes will be reflected in an updated policy and effective date. This policy makes no claim that optional services or external copies are deleted automatically.
